The Potluck Sign-Up Sheet

One master list, so nobody shows up with five trays of mashed potatoes and zero desserts. This page turns "just bring something!" into an actual, organized plan.

Getting started

  1. Print page 2 and jot what you're cooking yourself in the "Who's bringing" column first — the host's dishes go on the list like everyone else's.
  2. Copy the invitation blurb on page 3 into a family text or group chat. People reply with their dish; whoever holds the pen marks it claimed.
  3. Print page 3 for your own clipboard — it has the week-of confirmation checklist and an arrivals table, so you know who lands when and who needs an outlet.
  4. The "Serves" column is there so a rough headcount can do the worrying for you. Nobody is grading.

Rule of thumb for sizing: plan one main plus about one side dish for every two or three guests, and one dessert per six people. Two extras of anything is generous; five extras is a storage problem.

Free printable for personal use — print at 100% (Actual Size) on US Letter. Black-and-white printers are fine. No signups, no emails: this sheet never leaves your kitchen table.
