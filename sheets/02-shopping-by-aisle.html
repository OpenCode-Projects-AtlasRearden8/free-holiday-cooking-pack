The Shopping-by-Aisle Mega List

One giant grocery trip instead of four panicked ones. Everything is grouped the way stores actually are, so you walk the aisles in order and walk out ready.

Getting started

  1. Start on page 2 and fill the little planner box: guests, turkey size, and which extras you promised on the potluck sheet 01.
  2. Dash through the pre-printed items, cross out what you already own, and circle what you need — don't shop by memory, shop by page.
  3. Pages 2 and 3 are the aisles, in store order: produce, meat, dairy, bakery, pantry, spices, frozen, drinks, paper goods.
  4. The backup-plan box hides calmly at the bottom of page 3. Gaze at it if a store runs out of anything.

Turkey sizing cheat: buy about 1 lb of turkey per person, and closer to 1.25–1.5 lb per person if you're building in leftovers for sandwiches. That's the whole rule. (Sheet 03 tells you exactly when to start thawing it.)
